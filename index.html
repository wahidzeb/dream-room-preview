About Dream Room

Dream Room is a calm interior design game. Clients send you a short brief, and you pick the bed, walls, floor, art and more to match their style, colours and budget.

Each room is scored on style, colours, must-haves and completeness, with a Thrifty bonus for spending wisely. The story runs over five chapters, and your favourite rooms are kept in My Designs.

3 quick tips

  1. Check the must-haves first. Missing one caps you at 1 star.
  2. Look for the little heart on a piece. It means this client will like it. Then match the colour palette after each choice.
  3. Leave 15% of the budget unspent for the Thrifty bonus, but still fill every spot.
